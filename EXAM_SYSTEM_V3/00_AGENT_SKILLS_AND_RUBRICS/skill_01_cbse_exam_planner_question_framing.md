### 1. MISSION & THE ANTI-DAYDREAMING OBJECTIVE
The core mandate of this examination framework is **uncompromising cognitive verification**:
- Passive reading during daily timetable blocks easily masks mental distraction and daydreaming.
- The Host Examination engine enforces active recall, conceptual articulation, and step-by-step mathematical reasoning.
- Every question framed must test what was studied across three progressive tiers:
  1. **Knowledge & Recall (Bloom 1 & 2)**: Core definitions, mathematical axioms, terminology, SI units.
  2. **Application & Numerical Reasoning (Bloom 3)**: Formula manipulation, step-by-step problem solving, equation balancing.
  3. **Analysis & Source Interpretation (Bloom 4 & 5)**: Case-study scenarios, experimental diagrams, assertion-reasoning, cause-consequence historical links.

### 2. OFFICIAL CBSE SECTION ARCHITECTURE & QUESTION TYPOLOGIES

A standard CBSE Examination Question Paper is divided into five formal sections (`SECTION A` to `SECTION E`):

| Section | Typology | Mark Weight | Question Count (Full 80M) | Question Count (Sprint 25M) | Cognitive Focus |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---|
| **SECTION A** | Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ) & Assertion-Reason (A/R) | `[1]` each | 16 – 20 Qs | 5 – 6 Qs | Rapid concept verification, unambiguous single-choice answers |
| **SECTION B** | Very Short Answer (VSA) | `[2]` each | 6 Qs | 3 Qs | 2 distinct value points, direct definition, single-step numerical, or two differences |
| **SECTION C** | Short Answer (SA) | `[3]` each | 7 Qs | 3 Qs | 3 distinct value points, multi-step derivation, structured reasoning |
| **SECTION D** | Long Answer (LA) | `[5]` each | 3 – 4 Qs | 1 – 2 Qs | Exhaustive 5-point explanation, multi-part problem (3M + 2M), or diagram-integrated proof |
| **SECTION E** | Case-Based / Source-Based Integrated Task | `[4]` each | 3 Qs (sub-parts: 1+1+2 or 1+1+1+1) | 1 Q | Real-world scenario passage with data table/diagram and analytical sub-questions |

### 3. COMPETENCY-BASED QUESTION (CBQ) SPECIFICATIONS

At least **35% to 40%** of all test items must be Competency-Based, requiring genuine cognitive application rather than pure memorization:

#### A. Assertion-Reasoning (A/R) Protocol:
Every Assertion-Reason item presents two distinct statements:
- **Assertion (A)**: Factual or conceptual proposition.
- **Reason (R)**: Scientific, mathematical, or logical explanation.
- **Standard 4-Option Grid (Mandatory)**:
  - `(a)` Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true and Reason (R) is the correct explanation of Assertion (A).
  - `(b)` Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are true but Reason (R) is NOT the correct explanation of Assertion (A).
  - `(c)` Assertion (A) is true but Reason (R) is false.
  - `(d)` Assertion (A) is false but Reason (R) is true.

#### B. Case-Study / Source-Based Passage Protocol:
1. Provide a 100–150 word authentic scenario (e.g., drip irrigation setup, industrial chemical reaction, historical treaty excerpt, or real-life financial percentage calculation).
2. Sub-questions must test:
   - `(i)` Direct concept comprehension `[1]`
   - `(ii)` Application of formula/principle `[1]`
   - `(iii)` Analytical deduction, troubleshooting, or alternative internal choice `[2]`

### 4. SUBJECT-SPECIFIC QUESTION FRAMING RULES (ALL 7 SUBJECTS)

#### 1. Mathematics (*Ganita Prakash / NCERT Class 8*)
- **Algebra**: Algebraic expressions, identities $(a+b)^2, (a-b)^2, (a^2-b^2)$, factorization by grouping and common factors, linear equations in one variable with variable terms on both sides.
- **Mensuration & Geometry**: Quadrilaterals, parallelograms, trapeziums, surface area and volume of cylinders, cubes, and cuboids. Word problems must demand practical spatial visualization.
- **Internal Choices**: Provide internal choice ("OR") between two questions of the same chapter/topic and equal difficulty in Sections C and D.

#### 2. Physical Science (Physics & Chemistry)
- **Physics**: Force, pressure, friction, sound, light, electrostatics. Numericals must specify standard SI units ($\text{N}, \text{Pa}, \text{m/s}, \text{Hz}$). Ray diagrams must mandate incident ray arrows, reflected/refracted ray arrows, and normal lines.
- **Chemistry**: Synthetic fibres, coal & petroleum, combustion & flame. Chemical properties, ignition temperature, types of combustion (rapid, spontaneous, explosion), flame zones (inner dark, middle luminous, outer non-luminous).

#### 3. Biological Science (Biology)
- **Crop Production & Management**: Agricultural practices (soil prep, sowing, manure/fertilizer, irrigation methods: sprinkler vs. drip, harvesting, storage).
- **Microorganisms**: Friend & Foe (bacteria, fungi, protozoa, algae, viruses), nitrogen cycle, food preservation.
- **Cell Structure & Function**: Plant cell vs. animal cell, organelle functions (nucleus, mitochondria, chloroplasts, vacuoles). Explicitly demand labeled sketches with $\ge 4$ distinct labels.

#### 4. Social Science (*History, Geography, Civics*)
- **History (Our Pasts - III)**: Causal analysis using 3-node cause-event-consequence chains (e.g., British land revenue settlements: Permanent, Ryotwari, Mahalwari; Revolt of 1857 centers).
- **Geography (Resources & Development)**: Resource classification, soil conservation techniques (mulching, contour barriers, terrace farming), mineral types, agricultural patterns.
- **Civics (Social & Political Life - III)**: Indian Constitution, Fundamental Rights (Articles 14–32), Secularism, Parliament and Law-Making, Judiciary.

#### 5. English (*Poorvi / Honeydew & Grammar*)
- **Reading**: Unseen analytical passage (250–300 words) with vocabulary and inference questions.
- **Writing & Grammar**: Formal letter writing, Notice writing, Diary entry; tenses, active/passive voice, direct/indirect speech, sentence reordering.
- **Literature**: Textual theme analysis, extract-based poetry interpretation.

#### 6. Tamil (தமிழ்)
- **பகுதி அ (உரைநடை & செய்யுள்)**: திருக்குறள் (பொருளுரை, அணி விளக்கம்), மனப்பாடப் பாடல், உரைநடை வினா-விடை.
- **பகுதி ஆ (இலக்கணம்)**: மயங்கொலி எழுத்துகள் (ழ/ள/ல, ண/ந/ன, ர/ற), வல்லினம் மிகும் இடங்கள், வேற்றுமை உருபுகள்.
- **பகுதி இ (படைப்பாற்றல்)**: கடிதம் வரைதல், கட்டுரை எழுதுதல்.

#### 7. Hindi (हिंदी)
- **खंड क (अपठित बोध)**: गद्यांश पर आधारित प्रश्न।
- **खंड ख (व्याकरण)**: संज्ञा, सर्वनाम, विशेषण, क्रिया, काल, विलोम शब्द, पर्यायवाची, मुहावरे, संधि।
- **खंड ग (पाठ्यपुस्तक)**: पाठ आधारित लघु एवं दीर्घ उत्तरीय प्रश्न, कविता का भावार्थ।
- **खंड घ (रचनात्मक लेखन)**: पत्र लेखन, निबंध / अनुच्छेद लेखन।
