Q.9 Calculate maximum pressure exerted by wooden block 20x10x5 cm of mass 4 kg [3 Marks]
🔴 Tharun's Approach & Weakness:

"F = m*g = 4*10 = 40 N. Min Area = 10cm * 5cm = 0.10 * 0.05 = 0.005 m². P = F/A = 40 / 0.005 = 8000."
