### ### 1.1.1 Academic Profile & Branding Identification Header

#### #### 1.1.1.1 Student Identity, Class 8 CBSE & Senthil Public School Badging
* **Purpose**: Verifies that the active application session is calibrated specifically to Tharun's academic curriculum (Class 8 CBSE syllabus, term portions, and Senthil Public School Dharmapuri handbook standards). It anchors the student's personal ownership of the workstation.
* **Workflow**:
  1. Upon launching the application, observe the top-left title banner reading **THARUN'S SHINOBI HABIT BALANCER**.
  2. Next to the main title, confirm the glowing blue badge **CLASS 8**.
  3. Below the title, confirm the operational sub-header: **SENTHIL PUBLIC SCHOOL DIARY IMPORTER • TIMETABLE & FOCUS TIMER**.
  4. This header is informational and non-clickable; it serves as a visual guarantee that all underlying timing algorithms, subject gravity rules, and exam portions are strictly bound to Class 8 academic regulations.

#### #### 1.1.1.2 Offline Local Persistence Status Indicator
* **Purpose**: Guarantees that 100% of Tharun's study streaks, diary entries, focus logs, and examination files are stored directly on the local physical machine (`D:\THARUN PROJECT`), functioning with zero internet dependency and zero risk of cloud data loss.
* **Workflow**:
  1. Look directly beneath the main title bar to verify the local database connection status.
  2. All write operations (saving a diary item, extending a focus timer, or logging an exam score) immediately commit to local JSON files (`study_routine_data.json`, `tharun_diary_format.json`, `subject_study_ledger.json`).
  3. No login or network handshake is required; the application starts in local offline state immediately upon double-clicking the launcher.

### ### 1.1.2 Global Utility Controls Strip

The top-right corner of the title bar hosts four mission-critical global utility controls arranged horizontally:

```
[ 📅 October ▾ ]   [ 🧭 Glider Map ]   [ 🚨 HOST EXAM DUE: SUNDAY TEST ]   [ 🔥 Will of Fire: Day 4 Streak ]
```

#### #### 1.1.2.1 The **📅 Month Chakra Selector** Dropdown (30-Day Historical Month Swapper)
* **Purpose**: Allows Tharun and parents to navigate historical study records, timetable adherence metrics, and homework clearance across different calendar months without manual file editing.
* **Workflow**:
  1. Click on the dark navy button labeled **`📅 [Current Month Name] ▾`** (e.g., **`📅 October ▾`**).
  2. A styled dropdown menu appears displaying all months of the current academic year (June through March).
  3. Click on any target month.
  4. The application immediately updates the internal date filter: the 30-day consistency heatmap refreshes, the Study Ledger filters to the selected month, and historical homework archives for that month are surfaced.
  5. To return to the live session, click the dropdown again and select the current active calendar month.

#### #### 1.1.2.2 The **🧭 Glider Map** Master Architecture Hub Button
* **Purpose**: Provides instantaneous access to the complete 25-component system flowchart, plain-English subsystem explanations, and dependency maps directly inside the application. It ensures that neither student nor parent ever feels lost in the system architecture.
* **Workflow**:
  1. Click the glowing cyan button labeled **`🧭 Glider Map`** located immediately to the right of the month dropdown.
  2. The **ScholasticPulse Glider – Master Map & Directory** modal window immediately pops up on top of the main window.
  3. Use the search bar at the top of the modal to type any component tag (e.g., `[#12]` for Bedtime Guard, `[#08]` for Diary OCR, or `[#23]` for Evaluator).
  4. Read the exact purpose, inputs, outputs, and upstream/downstream connections for that component.
  5. Click **`✕ Close`** or press `Escape` to close the map and return to your study session.

#### #### 1.1.2.3 The **🚨 HOST EXAM DUE** Proactive Notification Beacon
* **Purpose**: An automated early-warning alert system that monitors Tharun's study schedule and ledger. When a Phase 1 Sunday Test, a Phase 2 3-Day Checkpoint, or a Phase 3 Exam-Eve Sprint is scheduled, this beacon lights up in glowing crimson to prevent missed revision deadlines.
* **Workflow**:
  1. If an exam is due on the active day, observe the glowing red button reading **`🚨 HOST EXAM DUE: [EXAM NAME]`** (e.g., **`🚨 HOST EXAM DUE: SUNDAY TEST`**).
  2. Hover over the badge to see the pending subject and mark allocation (e.g., *Mathematics – 35 Marks – 60 Mins*).
  3. Click the beacon directly with the mouse.
  4. The application automatically bypasses manual navigation and switches directly to **Tab 2 (Host Exam & AI)**, pre-selecting the exact exam blueprint ready for one-click generation.
  5. If no exam is due on the active calendar date, the beacon remains dormant/hidden, leaving the header clean and distraction-free.

#### #### 1.1.2.4 The **🔥 Will of Fire** Habit Streak & 7-Day Progress Dots Tracker
* **Purpose**: A psychological habit anchor based on the Naruto "Will of Fire" philosophy. It tracks Tharun's consecutive days of chair-time study and visualizes daily consistency across the current 7-day rolling week.
* **Workflow**:
  1. Observe the structured container at the far right of the header reading **`🔥 Will of Fire: Day X Streak`**.
  2. Directly underneath the streak count, inspect the 7 circular progress dots representing Monday through Sunday of the active week.
  3. **Solid Gold Dot (`●`)**: Indicates that Tharun successfully satisfied the daily evening study session and cleared his scheduled homework on that day.
  4. **Hollow Slate Dot (`○`)**: Indicates an upcoming or incomplete study day.
  5. Complete today's evening study schedule to watch today's hollow circle automatically convert into a glowing golden star/dot and increment the streak number by `+1`.

### ### 1.2.2 Right Deck: Matrix 4×3 Action Grid (Row 0: Configuration Selectors)

Row 0 of the right-hand command deck contains four pill-style selectors that configure the core behavior of the entire application:

```
[ ⚙️ MODE Selector ]    [ ⚡ LEVEL Selector ]    [ 🕒 START Selector ]    [ 🍥 SHINOBI Selector ]
```

#### #### 1.2.2.1 **⚙️ MODE**: Balanced Daily vs Pre-Exam Planner vs Exam Eve Sprint
* **Purpose**: Switches the scheduling algorithm between normal daily schooling, multi-day exam revision, and high-intensity test mornings.
* **Workflow**:
  1. Click on the **`⚙️ MODE`** pill selector card.
  2. A dark-glass selector panel opens presenting three distinct operational modes:
     * **☑️ Balanced Daily Mode**: Standard school evening routine (clearing diary homework, daily Maths problem-solving, diagram drawing, and bedtime at 10:00 PM).
     * **📑 Pre-Exam Planner Mode**: Multi-day horizon planner (configurable for 3-Day, 5-Day, or 7-Day pre-exam review periods with progressive chapter revisions).
     * **🎯 Exam Eve Sprint Mode**: High-priority cramming mode activated on the morning or evening of a school examination, strictly focusing on high-yield formulas and definitions.
  3. Select the desired mode. The daily timetable slabs below immediately reconstruct to reflect the selected operational focus.

#### #### 1.2.2.2 **⚡ LEVEL**: Level 1 (Genin 15m) vs Level 2 (Chunin 30m) vs Level 3 (Jonin 45m)
* **Purpose**: Calibrates the duration of individual focus intervals according to Tharun's current mental endurance, preventing attention fatigue on heavy school days.
* **Workflow**:
  1. Click on the **`⚡ LEVEL`** selector card.
  2. Choose between the three certified cognitive tiers:
     * **🔰 Level 1: Genin Academy (15m)**: 15-minute ultra-focused study sprints followed by 5-minute restorative breaks. Recommended when Tharun is tired after sports or long bus transit.
     * **⚡ Level 2: Chunin Shinobi (30m)**: Standard 30-minute deep-work intervals. The balanced default for Class 8 homework clearance.
     * **🔥 Level 3: Jonin Master (45m)**: 45-minute deep-focus blocks reserved for intensive mathematics problem-solving and full-length exam writing.
  3. Notice how all timetable slabs automatically re-slice their durations to match the chosen interval ceiling.

#### #### 1.2.2.3 **🕒 START**: 7:00 PM Static Evening Anchor vs ⚡ Auto-Fetch Live Time
* **Purpose**: Configures the start anchor of the evening timetable.
* **Workflow**:
  1. Click on the **`🕒 START`** selector card.
  2. Choose between two operational timing anchors:
     * **7:00 PM (Standard Anchor)**: Anchors the evening study session to start precisely at 7:00 PM, concluding at 10:00 PM.
     * **⚡ Auto-Fetch System Time**: Dynamically fetches the current live computer clock (e.g., 7:24 PM) and recalculates all subsequent homework slabs and breaks forward from this exact minute, while keeping the 10:00 PM bedtime curfew strictly intact.
  3. Use Auto-Fetch whenever Tharun starts studying earlier or later than the usual 7:00 PM anchor.

#### #### 1.2.2.4 **🍥 SHINOBI**: 8 Dynamic Character Palette Selector
* **Purpose**: Manually overrides the active character visual theme to match Tharun's personal preference for the day.
* **Workflow**:
  1. Click on the **`🍥 SHINOBI`** selector card.
  2. Select from the 8 full-color chakra palettes:
     * **Naruto Sage Mode**: Warm indigo-navy base with vibrant Sage orange and golden yellow accents.
     * **Sasuke Chidori**: Deep slate background with electric cyan chakra highlights.
     * **Kakashi Hatake**: Tactical stealth navy with Raikiri purple accents.
     * **Minato Flash**: Dark midnight obsidian with yellow-flash lightning amber.
     * **Itachi Uchiha**: Dark crimson Sharingan night palette.
     * **Leaf Village**: Konoha forest green chakra vitality theme.
     * **Modern Dark**: Clean, neutral distraction-free dark theme.
     * **High-Contrast Light**: Bright daytime reading palette.
  3. The entire application window immediately repaints with the selected theme's colors, borders, and fonts with zero flicker.

* **Purpose**: Provides immediate access to Tharun's catalog of documented academic slip-ups, arithmetic sign errors, and language spelling confusions (e.g., Tamil ழ/ள/ல distinctions or missing calculation units).

  1. Click the **`⚠️ Error Bank`** tile.
