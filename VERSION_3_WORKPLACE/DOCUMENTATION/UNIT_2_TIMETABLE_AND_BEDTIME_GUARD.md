### ### 2.1.1 Evening Routine Architecture (7:00 PM – 10:00 PM)

#### #### 2.1.1.1 Core Subject Efficiency Splitting (Maths, Science, Social 25m+ Sprint Halves)
* **Purpose**: Prevents attention residue and cognitive fatigue by eliminating runaway, monolithic study blocks (e.g., an unmanageable 60-minute or 78-minute block). Any core task requiring $\ge 25$ minutes is automatically bifurcated into two high-efficiency sprints.
* **Workflow**:
  1. When school diary homework indicates a heavy Mathematics or Science task (e.g., 40 minutes of algebraic exercise sums), the scheduler divides it into:
     * **Sprint 1 (Concept & Guided Step-Solving)**: Reviewing formulas, theorems, and teacher-worked textbook examples.
     * **Chakra Micro-Rest (4–5 mins)**: Brief cognitive rest to clear working memory.
     * **Sprint 2 (Independent Execution & Verification)**: Solving questions independently and framing answers with proper units.
  2. Each sprint is presented as an independent, checkable timetable card on Tab 0.

#### #### 2.1.1.2 Cognitive Interleaving: Analytical ➔ Linguistic ➔ Scientific
* **Purpose**: Protects adolescent neuro-endurance by alternating the cognitive processing domain between tasks rather than clustering similar subjects together.
* **Workflow**:
  1. The timetable sequencing algorithm ensures that Tharun's brain never tackles two heavy analytical subjects back-to-back.
  2. The schedule strictly interleaves:
     * **Analytical Domain (Maths)**: Numerical calculations, logical problem solving.
     * **Linguistic Domain (Tamil, English, Hindi)**: Reading comprehension, orthography, vocabulary.
     * **Scientific / Spatial Domain (Science, Social)**: Biological diagrams, physics ray optics, historical timelines.
  3. This rotation maintains mental alertness throughout the entire 3-hour evening window.

#### #### 2.1.1.3 Mandatory 15-Minute Blind Science Diagram & Map Drawing Slot
* **Purpose**: Enforces kinesthetic and visual retention through unskippable daily diagram drawing (ray diagrams, plant cell structures, circuit schematics, or historical map locations).
* **Workflow**:
  1. Locate the daily slotted card **`✍️ Mandatory Diagram & Map Drawing (15m)`** scheduled between 8:15 PM and 8:30 PM.
  2. Tharun opens a blank unruled drawing sheet.
  3. **The 3-Step Drawing Protocol**:
     * Step 1: Study the textbook diagram for 3 minutes.
     * Step 2: Close the book and draw the diagram completely from memory ("Blind Drawing") with pencil and ruler.
     * Step 3: Reopen the book and label all anatomical parts or circuit symbols in red ink to highlight omissions.
  4. Click the checkmark on the timetable card once completed.

#### #### 2.1.1.4 5–8 Minute Chakra Micro-Rest Breaks (Attention Residue Elimination)
* **Purpose**: Prevents mental exhaustion by inserting scheduled restorative intervals between subjects.
* **Workflow**:
  1. Between any two academic subjects, observe the scheduled green/cyan break card **`☕ Chakra Micro-Rest & Refresh`**.
  2. During this break, the student is instructed to stand up from the desk, hydrate, stretch, and step away from digital screens.
  3. When the countdown timer expires, an auditory chime sounds, signaling that Tharun's cognitive state is recharged for the next subject.

### ### 2.1.2 Morning Routine Architecture (6:00 AM – 7:30 AM)

#### #### 2.1.2.1 Morning Language & Orthography Clinic (Tamil ழ/ள/ல, Hindi मात्राएँ, English Grammar)
* **Purpose**: Capitalizes on morning mental freshness to eradicate recurring language mistakes, spelling slips, and handwriting fatigue without the stress of numerical calculations.
* **Workflow**:
  1. The morning routine activates automatically between 6:00 AM and 7:30 AM.
  2. The first 35-minute block is dedicated to the **Language Clinic**:
     * **Tamil Orthography**: 10 minutes practicing words containing tricky consonants (ழ, ள, ல, ண, ந, ன).
     * **Hindi Grammar**: 10 minutes writing sentences focusing on correct vowel diacritics (*matras*).
     * **English Creative Recall**: 15 minutes of independent paragraph writing or grammar rules.
  3. Practice prompts are dynamically extracted from **`student_error_bank.json`**.

#### #### 2.1.2.2 Prohibition of Heavy Mathematical Calculations in the Morning Window
* **Purpose**: Neurological protection rule: Complex multi-step arithmetic, long division, and algebraic derivations are strictly banned from the morning window before school to prevent morning frustration and anxiety before classes begin.
* **Workflow**:
  1. The scheduler algorithm enforces a hard filter: Mathematics homework items from the diary are **never** scheduled in the morning session.
  2. Mathematics is strictly reserved for the peak evening alertness window (7:00 PM – 8:30 PM).
  3. If a user attempts to manually force a math slot into the morning, the system displays an informational tip advising deferral to evening hours.

#### #### 2.1.2.3 Deferred Homework Catch-Up Processing
* **Purpose**: Provides a safe recovery zone for any low-priority reading or non-analytical homework tasks that were deferred from the previous night to protect Tharun's 10:00 PM sleep curfew.
* **Workflow**:
  1. If an evening session overran and a reading task was shifted past 9:45 PM, it automatically appears as a **Deferred Homework Slab** in the morning timetable.
  2. Tharun reviews and completes the reading material between 6:45 AM and 7:15 AM before packing his school bag.

## ## 2.2 Live Pomodoro Focus Timer & Interactive Controls

The Focus Timer is an interactive digital and audio countdown controller permanently linked to the currently active timetable slot.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        CORE MATHS SPRINT 1                             │
│                           [ 18 : 42 ]                                  │
│             ● WORK PHASE • TARGET: EXERCISE 9.1 (Q1 - Q4)             │
│                                                                        │
│   [ ▶ Start ]   [ ⏸ Pause ]   [ ⏹ Reset ]   [ ✅ Complete ]            │
│   [ +5m Extend ]   [ +10m Extend ]   [ +15m Extend ]   [ Custom... ]   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### ### 2.2.1 Visual HUD & Audio Bells

#### #### 2.2.1.1 Circular / Linear Countdown Clock with Phase Indicators
* **Purpose**: Gives Tharun a high-visibility, distraction-free visual anchor showing exactly how many minutes and seconds remain in the current sitting.
* **Workflow**:
  1. The top header of the timer card displays the active subject name and task goal.
  2. Below the title, large high-contrast numerical digits display the remaining time (`MM : SS`).
  3. Underneath the numbers, the **Phase Indicator Badge** glows:
     * **Orange / Gold (`● WORK PHASE`)**: Active deep-work study sprint.
     * **Emerald Green (`● REST PHASE`)**: Active micro-rest break.
  4. Click **`▶ Start`** to start counting down. Click **`⏸ Pause`** if interrupted. Click **`✅ Complete`** to mark the homework done ahead of time.

#### #### 2.2.1.2 Web Audio Bells & Character Jutsu Voice Notifications
* **Purpose**: Provides auditory cues so Tharun does not have to stare at the screen clock while solving problems on paper.
* **Workflow**:
  1. When the focus timer is launched, a crisp sound effect plays.
  2. When 60 seconds remain in a work sprint, a gentle reminder chime warns Tharun to finalize his current step.
  3. When the timer hits `00:00`, a full chime and character voice cue announce the transition: *"Chakra sprint complete! Stand up, stretch, and rest your eyes."*

---

### ### 2.2.2 Dynamic Time Extension System

#### #### 2.2.2.1 Quick-Add Extension Buttons (**+5m**, **+10m**, **+15m**, **Custom...**)
* **Purpose**: Real-world adaptability: Allows Tharun to extend a focus block when a challenging math problem requires a few more minutes to complete, without throwing off the rest of the night.
* **Workflow**:
  1. While the timer is running or paused, click any of the quick extension buttons:
     * **`[ +5m Extend ]`**: Adds exactly 300 seconds to the live countdown.
     * **`[ +10m Extend ]`**: Adds 600 seconds.
     * **`[ +15m Extend ]`**: Adds 900 seconds.
     * **`[ Custom... ]`**: Opens a small input box to type any number of minutes.
  2. The schedule immediately triggers the **Cascading Hierarchy Engine** to compensate for the added time without pushing bedtime past 10:00 PM.

#### #### 2.2.2.2 Reactive Timeout Prompt (Extend vs Complete vs Skip)
* **Purpose**: Prevents the application from running unattended if Tharun finishes late or steps away from the desk.
* **Workflow**:
  1. When the timer reaches `00:00`, an interactive modal automatically appears on top:  
     *“Time is up for Core Maths Sprint 1! Did you finish your task?”*
  2. Three clear action buttons are presented:
     * **`✅ Mark Done`**: Marks the slot completed and advances the schedule to the next break.
     * **`⏱️ Need 5 More Minutes`**: Instantly grants a 5-minute extension and logs the reason.
     * **`⏭️ Skip / Move to Next`**: Advances the schedule without extending.

## ## 2.3 The 10:00 PM Inviolable Bedtime Guard & Cascading Hierarchy

The Bedtime Guard is a strict pedagogical safety mechanism. Because sleep deprivation impairs memory consolidation and calculation accuracy in adolescent learners, the application enforces a hard, unbreachable ceiling: **all evening study must terminate at 10:00 PM**.

```
[ Focus Extension Granted (+15m) ]
                │
                ▼
  [ TIER 1: BUFFER COMPRESSION ] ────► Trims breaks from 10m down to 5m min.
                │
                ▼ (If still exceeding 10:00 PM)
  [ TIER 2: SECONDARY TRIMMING ] ────► Trims non-Math reading slots to 20m min.
                │
                ▼ (If time reaches 9:45 PM)
  [ TIER 3: MORNING DEFERRAL ]   ────► Moves remaining reading slot to 6:00 AM.
                │
                ▼
  [ 10:00 PM BEDTIME LOCKOUT ]   ────► Evening session terminates. Sleep safeguarded.
```

---

### ### 2.3.1 Tier 1: Buffer Compression (Trimming 10m Breaks down to 5m min)
* **Purpose**: Absorbs minor overruns by compressing non-study buffer time rather than sacrificing actual academic study minutes.
* **Workflow**:
  1. When a `+5m` or `+10m` extension is granted, the scheduler first inspects the remaining scheduled relief breaks.
  2. Standard 10-minute breaks are trimmed down to the minimum safe threshold of 5 minutes.
  3. If this compression absorbs the overrun, all study slots maintain their planned duration.

### ### 2.3.2 Tier 2: Secondary Trimming (Trimming Non-Math Reading Slots to 20m min)
* **Purpose**: Protects daily Mathematics problem-solving time when a major overrun occurs.
* **Workflow**:
  1. If Tier 1 buffer compression is insufficient to preserve the 10:00 PM curfew, the scheduler activates Tier 2.
  2. The scheduler locates secondary, non-analytical tasks (such as Social Science reading or English textbook reading).
  3. These reading slots are proportionally compressed down to a safe minimum of 20 minutes.
  4. Mathematics daily hand-solving is **never shortened**.

### ### 2.3.3 Tier 3: Morning Deferral (Automatic 9:45 PM Shift to 6:00 AM Slot)
* **Purpose**: Prevents late-night cramming when homework demands exceed evening hours.
* **Workflow**:
  1. At 9:45 PM, the scheduler conducts an automated curfew inspection.
  2. If an unfinished low-priority reading task remains, the application gracefully removes it from the evening timetable.
  3. The task is automatically re-slotted into tomorrow morning's 6:00 AM – 7:30 AM study window.
  4. A notification informs Tharun: *"Reading task deferred to 6:00 AM morning window to safeguard sleep. Bedtime is in 15 minutes."*

### ### 2.3.4 Bedtime Lockout (Strict 10:00 PM Study Ceiling Protection)
* **Purpose**: Enforces final shutdown of the evening study workstation.
* **Workflow**:
  1. At 10:00 PM sharp, the timetable shifts into **Sleep Mode**.
  2. The screen displays the Naruto Curfew Sleeping visual banner.
  3. The Focus Timer disarms, and the student is directed to complete their school bag check and retire to bed.
  4. Every extension, buffer trim, and deferral event is automatically compiled and written into **`time_extension_audit.json`** for parental review.
